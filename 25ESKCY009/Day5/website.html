About Us

Pravah is a student-led festival platform dedicated to empowering young voices, showcasing local talent, and building meaningful connections. We curate inclusive events that encourage participation, learning, and celebration.

Vision

To create a vibrant space where every student can explore their passions and share their talents with a supportive community.

Mission

To host engaging events, workshops, and performances that inspire personal growth, collaboration, and creative expression.

Values

Inclusivity, creativity, teamwork, and celebration of diversity are at the heart of everything we do.
